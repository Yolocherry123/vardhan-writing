Continuous Improvement

The electric light did not come from the continuous improvement of candles

— Oren Harari

Interpretation

Historians and designers note that real-world innovation is rarely a sudden magic trick. Electric lighting systems still relied on prior networks, engineering iterations, and existing subcomponents rather than appearing out of nowhere.
